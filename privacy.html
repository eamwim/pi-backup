Pi Backup privacy policy

Pi Backup is used only by its owner, to back up their own Raspberry Pi to their own Google Drive.

  • What it can access: only the Google Drive files and folders it creates itself (Google's drive.file permission). It cannot see any other file in the Drive.
  • What it stores: encrypted copies of the Raspberry Pi's files, in a folder in the owner's Drive. The encryption key stays with the owner, so the contents cannot be read in Drive.
  • What it shares: nothing. No data goes anywhere except the owner's Google Drive, and nothing is sold, shared or used for advertising.
  • Google user data is used only to make and restore these backups, in line with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
  • Removing access: remove "Pi Backup" under Third-party connections in the Google Account (myaccount.google.com/connections). Its files stay in the Drive until the owner deletes them.
